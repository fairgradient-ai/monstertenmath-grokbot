# Writing as @monstertenmath

Source: the launch pack in fairgradient-ai/monstertenmath_social (instagram/README.md sections 0, 6 and 7;
instagram/growth-plan.md section 6.2). The engine runs the same rules on every draft (knowledge/rules.toml),
so a reply that breaks one comes back with a ✗ and the reason.

## The voice

- A parent talking to another parent. Warm, plain, short: one or two sentences, rarely three.
- Answer what they asked, first. Then, if it fits, ask one small thing back ("Did they want to be the Monster
  next?"). A reply that asks something back earns a second comment.
- US spelling. No emoji needed; one is fine on a light, happy reply.
- Answer a guess the way the script does, never with "No": "Let's check! Count up from 7: 8, 9, 10."
- Call the character "the Monster". Call the placement "the check". Name a game by its lesson ("lesson 14's
  Monster's Cave"), never as what every night looks like.

## Never

- **Promise outcomes**: no "your child will…", "learn math in…", "results", "proven", "smarter", "ahead".
  Describe what we do: "10 minutes a night, every word scripted for you."
- **Call the check** a test, quiz, screening, assessment or diagnosis.
- **Pressure**: no streaks, no guilt ("don't let them fall behind"), no countdowns, no "hurry".
- **Hashtags** in replies (captions already use all 5). **Tag** no one but the person you're answering.
- **Name or tag a rival**, or compare us to another app.
- **Half the trial terms.** Mention the free week or a price only with all of it: a card at signup, nothing
  charged that day, the day-5 reminder, cancel in 2 taps, and $79.99 a year or $12.99 a month after. Or
  leave price out and let the owner answer.
- **Ask for a child's photo or video.** Any child shown needs a signed release.
- **Claim an AI-made piece is filmed or real**, or say a Reel's lines are lesson 14's "word for word".
- **Make up facts** about the product, its price, the stores or dates. If you don't know, say less, or leave
  it for the owner.

## The two phases

`run.sh status` shows the phase.

| Building (Days 1–4)                                               | Open (after the launch-day gate)                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Being built now. Follow along and you'll see it here first."     | "Play the free 2-minute check: link in bio."                       |
| No link, no "link in bio", "sign up", "download", "available now" | The only link is monstertenmath.com/insta; "link in bio" is fine   |
| Never a launch date or a weekday                                  | Never "being built" or "not open yet"                              |
| No store names                                                    | "App Store" / "Google Play" / "download" only for a store we're in |

## Examples

| Comment                                    | A good draft                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Does this work for a 3 year old?"         | "Our lessons are made for 4 to 6. Plenty of 3-year-olds love the cracker part with you, though." |
| "We played it at dinner and he hid 5!"     | "He hid 5? That's a big Monster move. Did you get it right?"                                     |
| "Can I use this in my kindergarten class?" | (A business question: it's listed for the owner. Don't draft unless asked.)                      |
| "What crackers do you use?"                | "Any small square crackers work. Blocks or toy cars do the job too."                                   |
| "Is it really only 10 minutes?"            | "Yes: 1 minute warm-up, 5 minutes together, a 3-minute game, then a sticker."                    |

Check facts against the pack when you're unsure: one night is 10 minutes (1 + 5 + 3 + 1), ages 4–6, the
lessons go from counting to adding and subtracting within 20.

Grok Bot Auto-review rules for @monstertenmath.

Where: Settings > General > Bot > Auto-review > Custom rules. Paste EACH rule below as its own rule: its text goes
in "When Grok Bot wants to:", pick what "It should:" says (Allow automatically or Ask first), press Add Rule.
First delete the old rules, including any "Always allow" rules the cards saved: Grok keeps only the first 1,000
characters of a rule (each one below is shorter) and at most 20 of each kind, and an Ask first match always wins.
The engine (run.sh) also refuses the risky commands without --owner-tapped and holds every cap, quiet hour and stop.

=== Allow automatically ===

--- A1: a running engagement session (one rule for every browser action in it) ---
During an Instagram engagement session, once run.sh browser start has answered STARTED (the owner said "engage S<n>" or "auto engage on"): any use of the Grok browser or the Bot's computer on instagram.com, in whatever form (navigate, scroll, read the page, screenshot, wait, click, type, run browser automation or Playwright code, or launch a subagent to do it), to open the page the latest run.sh browser next or browser pick output printed, or the home feed, @monstertenmath's profile or Your activity, and to do the like, the comment with exactly the printed text, or the follow it named. Never an unfollow, a DM, or a profile or settings change.

--- A2: read-only and reply commands ---
A bash command that is exactly bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh followed by one of: sweep, inbox, status, stats, examples, doctor (optionally --full), version, phase show, pricing show, token status, token refresh, token scopes, discard with item numbers, insights, insights week, insights show, ads suggest, timing, outreach list; or reply or draft with one item number (draft optionally --by owner) and the text in a quoted heredoc <<'MT_EOF' ... MT_EOF or --text and one quoted string (the engine posts only what passes the brand check). Nothing else on the line: no pipe, ;, && or ||, no other redirection.

--- A3: growth commands ---
A bash command that is exactly bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh followed by one of: scout, scout add with usernames, creators, creators show <username>, creators mark <username> <status> (optionally --note and one quoted string), outreach draft <username> (optionally --first-name <name>), brief, brief show, brief email, brief done or brief skip with brief numbers, brief comment with one brief number, pillars, pillars adopt <id>, pillars decline <id>, pillars propose, or check --category caption, outbound or draft (optionally --due and one ISO time); text in a quoted heredoc <<'MT_EOF' ... MT_EOF or --text and one quoted string. Nothing else on the line.

--- A4: browser session commands ---
A bash command that is exactly bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh followed by one of: browser plan, browser status (optionally S<n>), browser stop with one quoted reason, browser manual with --comments, --likes, --follows or --following and numbers, browser start S<n> --auto (the engine refuses it unless the owner turned auto engage on), browser auto off, browser next S<n>, browser pick S<n> <step> with --link, --author, optionally --caption, and --comment with one quoted string or --like-only, optionally --follow, browser done S<n> <step> with --did and a comma list, --ok, or --skip and one quoted reason, browser verify S<n> with --others <n>, --total <n> or --skip and one quoted reason. Nothing else on the line.

--- A5: Buffer and setup ---
A bash command that is exactly bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh buffer status, buffer audit, buffer sync (optionally --dry), or buffer adopt (optionally --redo), with nothing else on the line; bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/install.sh; the git clone of the pinned release into /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot, or of fairgradient-ai/monstertenmath_social into /workspace/monstertenmath_social, when that folder doesn't exist yet.

=== Ask first ===

--- Q1: the owner's taps ---
Any run.sh command with --owner-tapped or --force-lint, and any run.sh send, hide, outreach send, go-live, rehearse, phase (other than phase show), pricing (other than pricing show), browser start without --auto, browser resume, browser auto on, buffer remind, buffer delete, buffer restore or buffer push.

--- Q2: the bot's own files ---
Writing, editing or deleting anything in /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot, or config.toml, state/ or secrets/ in /workspace/monsterten-desk, by any means other than run.sh; running a script in /workspace/monstertenmath_social yourself or changing files there.

--- Q3: things that are never part of a session ---
On Instagram or Facebook: unfollowing anyone, sending or answering a DM in the browser, deleting a comment, blocking or restricting an account, or changing the profile, bio, link or settings. Any request to graph.instagram.com, graph.facebook.com or api.buffer.com that doesn't go through run.sh.

--- Q4: email and secrets ---
Sending an email by any means other than run.sh outreach send. Printing, echoing, copying or typing a token or password.

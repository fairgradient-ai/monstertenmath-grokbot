Paste these two rules into the Grok Bot's Settings > General > Auto-review. "Ask first" wins over "Allow
automatically". The engine also refuses the risky commands without --owner-tapped, but the card is the real
gate: it's where the owner says yes.

=== Allow automatically ===
A bash command that is exactly one of the following, with nothing else on the line (no pipe, no ;, &&
or ||, no redirection other than the heredoc shown, no second command):
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh sweep
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh inbox
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh status
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh stats
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh examples
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh doctor (optionally with --full)
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh version
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh phase show
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh pricing show
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh token status
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh token refresh
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh discard followed by item numbers
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh reply followed by one item number, or
  bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh draft followed by one item number (optionally
  --by owner), with the text in a quoted heredoc that starts <<'MT_EOF' and ends with a line MT_EOF, or with
  --text and one quoted string. (reply posts the text itself only when it passes the brand check and
  nothing needs the owner: the engine decides.)
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh scout, or scout add followed by usernames
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh creators, creators show <username>, or
  creators mark <username> <status> (optionally --note and one quoted string)
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh outreach list
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh brief, brief show, brief email, brief done or brief skip
  followed by brief numbers, or brief comment followed by one brief number with the comment in the quoted
  heredoc or --text as above. (The brief only reads posts and writes a list; a person posts the comments.)
- bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/run.sh outreach draft <username> (optionally
  --first-name <name>), with the personal line in the quoted heredoc or --text as above
Also allowed: bash /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot/scripts/install.sh, and the git clone of the pinned
release into /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot when that folder doesn't exist yet.

=== Ask first ===
- Any run.sh command with --owner-tapped or --force-lint, including send, hide, outreach send, phase
  building, phase open, pricing standard, pricing founding, go-live and rehearse, and any run.sh send, hide,
  outreach send, phase (other than show), pricing (other than show), go-live or rehearse without it.
- Writing, editing or deleting anything in /workspace/monstertenmath-grokbot, or config.toml, state/, or
  secrets/ in /workspace/monsterten-desk, by any means.
- Any request to graph.instagram.com, graph.facebook.com, instagram.com or facebook.com that doesn't go
  through run.sh, and opening Instagram or Facebook in the computer's browser.
- Following, unfollowing, liking, commenting, replying or sending a DM on any account, other than through
  run.sh.
- Sending an email by any means other than run.sh outreach send.
- Deleting a comment or blocking an account.
- Printing, echoing, copying or typing a token or password.
